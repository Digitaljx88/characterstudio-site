Character Studio Support

Need help or found a bug? Email characterstudio3dworkspace@gmail.com.

Please include your iPad model, your iPadOS version, the app's build number (Settings > About) and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot or screen recording helps.

Common questions

Where are my projects?

They're stored on your iPad, in the app's own storage. You can also see them in the Files app under Character Studio.

How do I back up my work?

Copy your projects out of the Character Studio folder in the Files app, or include them in an iPad backup. Do this before deleting the app, because deleting it can delete its projects.

Does the app need an account or internet connection?

No. There's no account, and your work stays on your device.

Does it collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
