Character Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Character Studio works on your device. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your projects or any other data anywhere. We do not collect your data.

Your projects and settings

Your projects and settings stay in the app's own storage on your iPad. They are visible in the Files app under Character Studio, and iPad backups can include them. Deleting the app can delete this storage, so export anything you want to keep first.

Photos and files

Pictures and files reach the app only when you pick them, using the system's Photos and Files pickers. The app can't browse your photo library. Exports leave the app only when you choose where they go.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads and any TestFlight use under its own privacy policy. If you share diagnostics with developers through your device settings, Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you directly.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to reply, and we don't sell or share it.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
